About N8iV Promotions

We Make Marketing Visible
All the Way to Revenue.

N8iV Promotions helps businesses turn SEO, social content, ads, CRM activity, and reporting into one decision system. ARIE is the engine we use to connect the work to revenue.

Our Philosophy

Marketing should create decisions, not just activity.

Most marketing gets judged in pieces. SEO reports talk about rankings, social reports talk about reach, ad reports talk about clicks, and CRM reports talk about deals. The business still has to guess how those pieces connect.

N8iV Promotions was built to close that gap. We combine practical marketing execution with revenue intelligence so every channel can be understood in context: what created attention, what created pipeline, and what helped revenue close.

ARIE, the Automatic Revenue Intelligence Engine, is our proprietary operating layer for attribution and reporting. It gives us a repeatable way to connect source data, spot gaps, select the right attribution model, and turn the findings into recommendations a business can act on.

Methodology

Four principles guide every engagement. They keep the work practical, measurable, and tied to the decisions your business needs to make next.

01

Revenue Context First

We start with business goals, sales cycle, CRM structure, and revenue quality before choosing what to measure or which attribution model to use.
