08 / 08Before you commitAct 3 · Path

Clear scope. Zero mystery.

The pilot is designed to answer one real budget question without locking your team into a long engagement.

What do we need before the pilot?

Active B2B marketing spend, a CRM or reliable opportunity record, and permission to review agreed exports or scoped access.

Your data stays yours.

  • Read-only. We can look at your data. We can’t change it, move it, or spend a dollar of it.
  • We never touch your money. We never touch your ad campaigns, your bank accounts, or your money. Ever.
  • Prefer exports? If you’d rather send agreed data exports than grant access, that works exactly the same.
  • Nothing is shared, sold, or reused. Your data is never used for anyone else’s marketing.
  • Delete on request. Ask us to delete everything we hold and we’ll confirm it in writing.
What if our tracking is incomplete?

That is often the most valuable finding. Missing, inferred, and confirmed evidence are separated so you know what can be trusted and what must be repaired.

Will you tell us if we are not ready?

Yes. If the available data cannot support a responsible attribution report, N8iV will say so before you spend a dollar.

When do we pay?

Payment is due once you sign the pilot proposal.

How fast is the pilot report?

Once we receive your data access or exports, your Revenue Receipt is completed within 24 hours.

What happens after the report?

You can act on the recommendations internally or discuss ongoing intelligence support. There is no obligation to continue.

The $750 covers the complete pilot. If you want ongoing monthly intelligence reports afterward, we’ll quote that separately — no obligation, no auto-renewal.
