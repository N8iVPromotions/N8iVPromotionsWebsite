A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Clear from
the beginning.

Is this another dashboard?+

The core deliverable is a Revenue Receipt: a finding, the evidence behind it, its limitations, and a recommended next step. Visuals support the decision.

What if our tracking is incomplete?+

Start with the self-audit. In a Fit Review, we can assess whether enough evidence exists for a pilot. If the foundations need work first, those gaps become the next steps.

Which tools do you work with?+

Meta Ads, HubSpot, and Stripe are the current core stack. Other sources and export-based workflows can be discussed during a Fit Review.

Does attribution prove an ad caused revenue?+

No. Attribution connects observed touchpoints with outcomes under a defined model. Causal lift requires a different measurement approach. We keep that distinction visible, along with the difference between closed-won deals and collected cash.

Do I need to connect my accounts for the self-audit?+

No. The self-audit uses 15 self-reported answers to generate your readiness score. It does not connect to your business accounts. You can choose to share your results when requesting a Fit Review. See our privacy policy for measurement and data-handling details.

What happens after the pilot?+

You receive the scoped report and recommendations. If ongoing analysis would help, a monthly engagement can be discussed and quoted separately. The pilot does not start a subscription.
