A few things worth knowing

Is this another dashboard?

The core deliverable is a Revenue Receipt: a finding, the evidence behind it, its limitations, and a recommended next step. Visuals support the decision.

What if our tracking is incomplete?

Start with the self-audit. A Fit Review assesses whether enough evidence exists for a pilot. If the foundations need work first, those gaps become the next steps.

Which tools do you work with?

Meta Ads, HubSpot, and Stripe are the current core stack. Other sources and export-based workflows can be discussed during a Fit Review.

Does attribution prove an ad caused revenue?

No. Attribution connects observed touchpoints with outcomes under a defined model. Causal lift requires a different measurement approach. Closed-won deals and collected cash stay separate.

Do I need to connect accounts for the self-audit?

No. The self-audit uses 15 self-reported answers. It does not connect to your business accounts, and no email is required to see your score.

What happens after the pilot?

You receive the scoped report and recommendations. If ongoing analysis would help, a monthly engagement can be discussed and quoted separately. There is no automatic subscription.
