08 / 08Before you commit5 questions

Clear scope. Zero mystery.

The pilot is designed to answer one real budget question without locking your team into a long engagement.

What do we need before the pilot?

Active B2B marketing spend, a CRM or reliable opportunity record, and permission to review agreed exports or scoped access.

What if our tracking is incomplete?

That is often the most valuable finding. Missing, inferred, and confirmed evidence are separated so you know what can be trusted and what must be repaired.

Will you tell us if we are not ready?

Yes. If the available data cannot support a responsible attribution report, N8iV will say so before you spend a dollar.

When do we pay, and how fast is the report?

Payment is due once you sign the pilot proposal. The report is completed within 24 hours of receiving your data access or exports.

What happens after the report?

You can act on the recommendations internally or discuss ongoing intelligence support. There is no obligation to continue.
