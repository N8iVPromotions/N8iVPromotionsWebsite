Privacy Policy

This policy explains how N8iV Promotions collects and uses information submitted through this website, including audit requests and newsletter subscriptions.

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit a revenue audit request, we collect the details you provide, such as name, work email, company, role, monthly marketing investment range, CRM platform, and your marketing visibility challenge.

When you subscribe to the newsletter, we collect your email address and the page where the subscription was submitted.

We may also receive basic technical information from hosting, analytics, security, and email delivery tools, such as IP address, browser type, device information, referral source, and page activity.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to audit requests and business inquiries.
  • Evaluate whether a pilot engagement is a good fit.
  • Send requested newsletter or marketing updates.
  • Improve the website, reporting, content, and service experience.
  • Protect the site from spam, abuse, and unauthorized use.

Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers that help operate the website, process form submissions, deliver email, host the site, or manage analytics. These providers are used only to support N8iV Promotions operations.

Newsletter Choices

You can request removal from N8iV Promotions email updates at any time by contacting zajen@n8ivpromotions.com.

Data Retention

We keep inquiry and subscription information only as long as reasonably needed for business communication, service delivery, recordkeeping, security, and compliance purposes.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and operational safeguards to protect information submitted through the website. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed as completely secure.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted by emailing zajen@n8ivpromotions.com. We may need to retain certain records where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact N8iV Promotions at zajen@n8ivpromotions.com.

This page is provided for transparency and is not legal advice. N8iV Promotions may update this policy as the website, services, and data practices evolve.
