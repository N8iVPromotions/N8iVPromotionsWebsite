04 / 06How it worksAct 3 · Path

Four steps from access to a decision.

01 / SCOPE

Confirm the decision

We define the budget question and 30-day analysis window, then agree on systems, access, limits, and delivery timing after confirming data readiness. The analysis window is not a delivery deadline.

02 / RECONCILE

Connect the evidence

N8iV normalizes your marketing, CRM, and revenue signals, and flags gaps before any conclusion is drawn.

03 / MATCH

Match payments to deals

Every payment is matched to a deal, marked unresolved, or excluded, with the reason. A person reviews every match; nothing is matched automatically.

04 / DECIDE

Deliver the Receipt

You get what the evidence supports, what is still unresolved, what to fix first, and the next step for your budget decision.

06 / 06Before you commitAct 3 · Path

Straight answers.

What if the data can't support a confident answer yet?

Then the Receipt says so. Money we can't link stays unresolved instead of being assigned to a channel, and the Receipt lists what to fix so next month can show more.

Isn't attribution just correlation?

Credit isn't proof, and we don't present it as proof. The Receipt reports which payments link to which tracked deals. It doesn't claim a channel caused the revenue.

Will you tell us if we're not ready?

Yes. If your data can't support a responsible decision, N8iV will say so before you spend a dollar.
