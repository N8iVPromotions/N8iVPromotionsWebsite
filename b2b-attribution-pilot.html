The problem

Your platforms report conversions. Your budget needs evidence.

01 / SOURCE

Campaign context breaks

UTMs, click IDs, forms, and CRM records often stop agreeing before a lead becomes an opportunity.

02 / CREDIT

Every platform claims the win

Each ad platform optimizes inside its own view, which creates duplicated or conflicting credit.

03 / DECISION

You set the budget anyway

Every month someone decides where the money goes, usually from platform-reported conversions and a gut call.

What you receive

A decision brief, not another dashboard login.

N8iV reconciles the evidence you authorize. The brief tells you what the data supports, how strongly, and what to do next.

  • A data-quality assessment, and how it affects confidence
  • Spend and revenue reconciled by channel
  • Attributed revenue per channel, with a confidence range
  • A model stress test: does the answer hold across first-touch, last-touch, and multi-touch views?
  • Unattributed and conflicting revenue, and what it would take to resolve it
  • A recommended budget split: scale, test, repair
  • One designed test, with what it will tell you and when
  • An executive-ready final brief

How it works

Four steps from access to a decision.

01 / SCOPE

Confirm the decision

We define the budget question, size the spend window to your sales cycle, and agree on the systems, access method, and limits.

02 / RECONCILE

Connect the evidence

N8iV normalizes your marketing, CRM, and revenue signals, and flags gaps before any conclusion is drawn.

03 / ESTIMATE

Measure what each channel produced

You get attributed revenue per channel, a confidence range on each number, and a check on whether the answer holds across models.

04 / DECIDE

Deliver the brief

You get what to scale, what to test, and what to repair, plus the design for your first test.

After the pilot

Month two starts with an answer on the way.

The test from your pilot resolves over the following weeks. On a monthly engagement, each result updates your estimates and your next budget recommendation, so confidence grows every cycle. Your pilot fee is credited to your first month, and there's no obligation to continue.

Before you commit

Straight answers.

What if the data can't support a confident answer yet?

Then the brief says so and shows the range. A wide range tells you where not to make big moves yet, and the designed test is how the range narrows.

Isn't attribution just correlation?

Credit isn't proof, and we don't present it as proof. Where the stakes justify it, the brief includes a test that measures a channel's effect directly.

Will you tell us if we're not ready?

Yes. If your data can't support a responsible decision, N8iV will say so before you spend a dollar.
