N8iV Promotions | Revenue Receipt outline

This is an illustrative report outline, not a client result.

1. The business question
One focused question, agreed before analysis.

2. Analysis window
The last complete month of ad spend. Delivery timing is agreed after data readiness is confirmed.

3. Sources and definitions
Marketing exports, CRM records, and available revenue records.
Document identifiers, source continuity, deal stages, and the distinction between closed-won value and collected cash.

4. Finding
State what is known, what is inferred, and what remains unresolved.

5. Evidence
Attach a clear source trail, relevant records, the attribution model, and exclusions.

6. Limits and gaps
Describe missing identifiers and records. Observed attribution does not establish causal lift.

7. Next decision
A recommendation to scale, test, or repair, with the evidence beside it.
